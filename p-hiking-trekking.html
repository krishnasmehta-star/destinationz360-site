What this is

School hiking and trekking expeditions

A trek is the one programme where optimism is dangerous. A route graded on how it looks rather than on who is walking it produces a group strung out over two kilometres at dusk, which is how trekking incidents begin.

So the grading conversation happens early and bluntly. We ask about the least fit student, not the most. Altitude profiles include acclimatisation days that cannot be skipped for convenience, and the evacuation route and nearest medical facility are written into the plan for every night of the itinerary.

Before anything else

A school buys supervision before it buys a destination.

Every programme on this site runs to the same written protocol. It is published in full, because a teacher who is personally accountable should not have to take it on trust.

  • A named supervision ratio for this activity and this year group, in the proposal.
  • Headcounts at every transition, recorded against a written list.
  • Certified instructors and their own equipment, or the activity is removed.
  • An evacuation route and named facility written for every night.
