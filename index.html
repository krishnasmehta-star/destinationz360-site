How we run a tour

The part of a school trip nobody puts on the brochure.

A school trip is signed off by someone who is personally accountable if a child is hurt. So the protocol is published in full, not described as experienced staff.

  • A named supervision ratio, written into the proposal before you commit.
  • Headcounts at every transition, recorded against a list, not done by eye.
  • A night protocol that does not flex on the last night.
  • Certified instructors for every adventure activity, or the activity is cut.
  • An evacuation route and named facility for every night of the trip.
  • One coordinator with the group, and a written note home each day.

How it works

Four steps, and a person at every one.

The same sequence whether it is a day out for one class or three weeks abroad for a year group.

01

You tell us the objective

One sentence about what the class is meant to understand, the year group, and a rough window. Budget is the fourth question, not the first.

02

We send a costed plan

Route, nights, drive times, room splits, dietary counts, the supervision ratio and the wet-weather alternative. On one document the school can circulate.

03

The protocol is agreed

Consent, medical declarations, insurance and the contingency plan settled in writing before a single deposit moves.

04

A coordinator travels with the group

First pick-up to last drop, holding the counts, the documents and the changes. The school gets one number, including at two in the morning.
