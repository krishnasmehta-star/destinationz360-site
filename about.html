Who we are

The school travel brand of Stuti Tourism.

Destinationz 360 plans and operates educational travel for schools and institutions. It sits inside Stuti Tourism Pvt. Ltd., which has been designing travel since 2009 and has served more than 800,000 travellers across its brands.

Zubilant, the group’s private travel brand, is our sibling. Same company, same operating relationships, same people answering the phone. What differs is the discipline a school trip demands: consent and medical documentation, supervision ratios, headcount procedure, curfew, acclimatisation, activity restrictions and a named person accountable for all of it.

That discipline is not a marketing position. It is written down as an operational rulebook, and the public version of it is on How we run a tour. We would rather be judged on that page than on this one.

How we work

Four habits. They explain most of our decisions.

None of them are original. Applying them to every trip, including the cheap one-day one, is the difficult part.

01The objective is written firstOne sentence from the teacher about what the class should understand. Everything downstream is judged against it, including whether a famous viewpoint earns its two hours.
02Say the number you can defendNo published prices, no rounded-up figures, no borrowed ratings. Where a fact is missing this site shows a gap rather than a plausible substitute.
03The protocol does not flexCurfew on the last night is the curfew on the first. An acclimatisation day stays in the plan when the schedule is tight. This is the whole job.
04One person, reachableA coordinator travels with the group and answers the phone at two in the morning. A helpline is not a substitute and never has been.

What this site does not claim

The things we have deliberately left blank.

A school tour website is an easy place to round a number up. Rather than do that, here is what is missing and why, so that nothing on the rest of the site has to be taken on faith.

Schools served, students travelled, and years running school tours. The 2009 date and the 800,000 travellers figure belong to Stuti Tourism as a group and are attributed that way throughout. Destinationz 360 has its own numbers and they are not published here until they have been confirmed rather than estimated.
Named schools and testimonials. We have not printed a client list or a quotation. A trip coordinator’s own words are the single most useful thing on a page like this, which is exactly why inventing one would be indefensible.
Photographs of our own groups. Every image on this site is a destination photograph from the group library. Correct and licensed, but not a single one shows a Destinationz 360 school group, and they are being replaced.
Ratings. Zubilant’s Google rating belongs to Zubilant. It is not transferred to this brand, because a parent checking our reputation deserves to be checking ours.
