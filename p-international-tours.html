What this is

International educational tours for schools

An international school tour is not a domestic one with a flight attached. Passports and visas have lead times that do not care about the school calendar, minors travelling without parents need specific consent documentation, and a medical situation abroad is a different problem entirely.

We start these early, and we start with the paperwork rather than the destination, because the paperwork is what decides whether the destination is possible. The itinerary conversation is more enjoyable, which is precisely why it should not be first.

Classes 8 to 12IB and international curriculaExchange and competition travel

  • Documentation timeline first Passport validity, visa lead times, consent for minors and the deadlines working backwards from departure.

  • Headcount discipline at every transition, recorded rather than remembered.
  • A costed plan showing the logistics, before the school commits.

Before anything else

A school buys supervision before it buys a destination.

Every programme on this site runs to the same written protocol. It is published in full, because a teacher who is personally accountable should not have to take it on trust.

  • A named supervision ratio for this activity and this year group, in the proposal.
  • Headcounts at every transition, recorded against a written list.
  • Certified instructors and their own equipment, or the activity is removed.
  • An evacuation route and named facility written for every night.
